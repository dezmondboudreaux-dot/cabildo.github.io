⦿ DECENTRALIZED GOVERNANCE ⦿

One member, one vote · Soulbound token · Annual plenary

The Mandala Foundation is a member‑managed Wyoming Decentralized Unincorporated Nonprofit Association (DUNA). All governance authority rests with active members. The DAO (Decentralized Autonomous Organization) implements binding votes on budget, platform amendments, and election of the Directorate (Executive Committee).

⚡ SELF‑SOVEREIGN

🔹 SOULBOUND MEMBERSHIP TOKEN

Non‑transferable ERC‑721 token on Arbitrum. One token = one vote. Minted after passing examination and completing the Five Pecks pledge.

Contract: 0x7D5…F3A9 (placeholder)

🗳️ GASLESS

🔹 SNAPSHOT VOTING

All binding votes occur on Snapshot. Proposals include budget approval, platform amendments (75% supermajority), and election of the 2‑3 member Directorate.

→ VIEW SPACE
🏛️ ANNUAL

🔹 MEMBERSHIP MEETING

The Great Kurultai (Annual Meeting) is held once per year, physical or digital. Binding votes only at this meeting, except special petitions.

Quorum: 10% for budget, 25% for amendments & directorate election.

🔐 MULTISIG

🔹 SAFE TREASURY

On‑chain treasury (Arbitrum) controlled by a Safe multisig. Signers are Directorate members. All expenditures follow DAO‑approved budget.

Current NAV: $142,000 USDC (placeholder)
