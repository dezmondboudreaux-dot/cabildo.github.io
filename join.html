⦿ FREQUENTLY ASKED

Q: Is the $20 pledge required every year?
A: Yes. Membership remains active only while your pledge is current. You will receive renewal reminders.

Q: Can I pay in cryptocurrency?
A: Yes. USDC (Arbitrum) preferred. Fiat options are available via our fiscal sponsor – contact us for details.

Q: What if I fail the exam?
A: You may retake after 30 days. Study materials are available in the Network Hub.

Q: Do I have to join an oba (housing cooperative)?
A: Not immediately. Membership is individual. Obas are encouraged but optional.
